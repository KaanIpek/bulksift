Privacy Policy

BulkSift · last updated 16 August 2026

BulkSift recognises trading cards entirely on your device. No photo, no video frame, and no image of any card ever leaves your phone. There is no server that sees your camera.

That is not a promise about how we handle your data — it is a description of how the app is built. Recognition runs against a card index bundled inside the app, which is why scanning works with no signal at all.

The camera

While the scanner is open, frames from the camera are read into memory, compared against the bundled index, and discarded. Nothing is written to disk, nothing is added to your photo library, and nothing is uploaded. Closing the scanner ends this immediately.

Your collection

Cards you scan or add are stored in a file on your device. If you never create an account, that file is the only copy and we cannot see it.

If you do create an account, your collections are copied to our sync service so a second device can show the same cards. You can delete that copy at any time from Settings → Account → Delete account, which removes your account and the synced data. The cards on your phone are deliberately left alone — deleting an account should not destroy an evening of scanning.

What is actually collected

Analytics Never No analytics or crash-reporting SDK is linked into the app.

Advertising and tracking

Watching a video is always your choice, and it earns scans. Before the first ad is requested, iOS asks whether BulkSift may track you. Declining changes nothing about what you get — the same scans are granted for the same watched video; the ads are simply less relevant.

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use the advertising identifier if you allowed tracking. Google's practices are described in their privacy policy.

Prices

Card prices are downloaded as a single public file. That request carries no account, no identifier and no information about your collection — the app asks for a file and gets a file.

Children

BulkSift is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

If you have an account you can delete it, and everything synced with it, from inside the app. If you would rather ask us, or want a copy of what is stored, write to support@bulksift.app and we will action it within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected, the app will say so before the change takes effect. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
