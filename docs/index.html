BulkSift

Turn a pile of trading cards into a priced list.

Prop your phone over the table and pass cards under it. Each one is recognised as it goes by — there is no shutter button and no waiting between cards. A bulk box that takes an evening to enter by hand takes a few minutes.

Runs on your phone

20,444 cards ship inside the app. Nothing is uploaded, and it works with the wifi off — at a card show, in a basement, at the back of a shop.

Prices you can check

Refreshing re-prices the cards you scanned last week too, so a collection total is a number you can act on.

Built for piles

Several collections, a feed you can correct in one tap, manual search, a want list, and a value history.

What it costs

Scanning is free every day, and watching a short video adds more scans. Pro removes the limit and unlocks CSV export. Scan credits can also be bought outright, with no subscription — a one-off box does not need one.
