Support

BulkSift · support@bulksift.app

Email support@bulksift.app and you will get a reply. Telling us the card that went wrong — the name, the set, and what the app said instead — is worth more than any other detail.

A card was read as the wrong one

Tap the row in the scan feed. It offers the next-closest matches, and picking one corrects the entry and gives you the scan back. If the card is a full art or a heavy holo, hold it a moment longer: the app combines several frames to see past the glare, and it needs those frames.

Nothing is being recognised

Fill more of the frame with the card, and avoid a lamp reflecting straight back at the lens. A matte surface underneath helps more than brighter light. Sleeved cards are fine — the app learns the offset your sleeves introduce after a few cards.

Prices look out of date

Settings → Prices → Refresh prices now. That re-prices everything you have already scanned, not just the next card.

I paid and Pro is not showing

Settings → Purchases → Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to the Apple ID that made them, so make sure you are signed in as that one.

Deleting your account

Settings → Account → Delete account. That removes your account and the synced copy of your collections. The cards on your phone are left alone on purpose.

Does it need an account or a signal?

No to both. Recognition, prices and your collection are all on the device. An account exists only so a second device can show the same cards.
